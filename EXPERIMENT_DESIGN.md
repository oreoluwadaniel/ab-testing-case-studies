# Experiment Design Notes

## Decision sequence

```text
Business question
      |
      v
Primary metric
      |
      v
Treatment and control definition
      |
      v
Minimum detectable effect
      |
      v
Power and sample-size check
      |
      v
Statistical test
      |
      v
Confidence interval and effect size
      |
      v
Business decision
```

## Rules used in the case studies

- The primary metric is defined before looking at the result.
- The analysis distinguishes statistical significance from practical importance.
- Intent-to-treat is used where the experiment design requires it.
- Multiple comparisons are adjusted where several hypotheses are tested.
- Power and minimum detectable effect are checked before treating a null result as evidence of no effect.

## Important limitation

The experiments use synthetic data. The results demonstrate the analysis method, not a claim about a real product or customer population.
